这是什么

hookjudge 是管道背后的判官：进来一条标准化的事件，出去一个判定（重要程度、类型、一句话摘要），送回管道，并记进账本。它不画卡片、不认识任何渠道，那是管道的事；它也不做深入调查，那是调查员的事。判官只回答一个问题：这条告警值不值得打扰一个人。

它还会说明这个判定配得上哪些按钮：一个按它刚判的严重程度定时长的「静默」，以及「值得叫醒 / 不值得」这一对。恢复通知不带按钮，因为已经没什么可静默的了。按钮背后的签名令牌由管道铸造，按下后也由管道接住，所以这个服务不持有任何密钥。

四条路径就是成本策略，顺序本身就是策略

路径什么时候走花钱吗
recovery 恢复恢复通知直接继承它那次触发得到的判定，关键是和它所属的告警保持一致，而不只是省一次调用免费
reuse 复用同一个条件在窗口内（默认 1 小时）重复出现，直接沿用上一次 AI 的判定：告警风暴只是一件事重复了很多遍免费
ai真正新的条件，问模型付费
rule 规则模型没配、太慢或不可用时的关键词兜底；degraded_reason 写明为什么降级，从不假装是分析。已知的级别会保守地沿用免费

省钱不是靠更便宜的模型，而是靠大多数事件根本走不到 ai 这一步。

这个页面怎么读

概览最上面一句话告诉你有没有事要你处理：判定没能送回管道是最要紧的，其次是等你复核的分歧。旁边那条横条就是成本策略：每条判定走了哪条路径、花了多少。

下面六个数字：过去 24 小时的判定数（也就是打扰了人多少次）、花费和付费占比、送回失败（大于 0 说明管道收不到判定；设置了 HOOKJUDGE_ALARM_URL 时，这个消息会绕开管道直接报警）、与平台一致（影子部署里，管道把上游平台自己的判断作为 level 一起送来，账本就能算出判官和平台多常一致；恢复通知不算，因为它们的重要程度按设计是继承的）、重复打扰和有人说有用。

最吵的条件是要动手的地方：每个打扰了不止一次的条件，打扰了几次、几次付了费、有人按过几次「值得」。打扰很多、从没人说有用的那一行，就是该去关掉点什么的地方。这里不会压制任何东西：判官仍然返回每一个判定，这只是把账算清楚。

判定页按天列出每一条判定：左边是重要程度，右边是走的路径和时间，点一行从右侧看完整内容。复核页列出平台和判官说法不一的判定，每点一次就是一条评估标签，可以导出。

打扰和花费是两本不同的账

每个判定都会回到管道变成一张卡片，所以判定数一直就是打扰人的次数，只是以前被当成吞吐量来读。一小时里同一个条件被判了十二次，就打扰了人十二次；其中十一次走的是免费的复用，所以花费数字对此只字不提。重复打扰就是这隐藏的部分。有人说有用是另一半：当有人按下卡片上的按钮，「值得 / 不值得」会回到 POST /feedback，记在引起那次打扰的判定上。这和复核问的不是一个问题：一个判得完全正确的 high，也可能不值得叫醒谁。

接口和地址

在 docker 网络里用 http://hookjudge:8200；在本机用 http://127.0.0.1:8200。

接口用途
POST /events管道送来的标准化事件（可选时间戳 HMAC 签名）。立刻回 202，判定从回流门送回去，不占着连接
POST /feedback有人按了卡片上的按钮，由管道去掉渠道信息后转来（和 /events 同样的签名和密钥）。「值得 / 不值得」记在判定上；「静默」是对管道的请求，这里不记裁定，因为「让它别响」不等于「它不重要」
GET /status · GET /disagreements这个页面读的数据（读取口令）
POST /judgements/{id}/label · GET /labels/export记一条复核标签；导出全部标签
GET /live页面的更新通知：每落一条判定推一次 changed，所以页面不用定时刷新
GET /healthz · GET /metrics存活检查；Prometheus 指标

要紧的几个设置

环境变量含义
HOOKJUDGE_AI_BASE_URL / _API_KEY / _MODEL任何兼容 OpenAI 的接口。留空的话每个事件都落在规则兜底上：能用但粗糙，而且永远不会发生复用
HOOKJUDGE_REUSE_WINDOW_SECONDS复用窗口，默认 3600
HOOKJUDGE_RETURN_URL / _RETURN_SECRET唯一的出口：管道的 judge-notify 门。失败会退避重试，重试用完进死信并自报警
HOOKJUDGE_RETENTION_DAYS · HOOKJUDGE_ALARM_URL账本保留天数，默认 30；死信自报警发往哪里

页面不设时钟：它和 /live 保持一条流式连接，服务说有判定落地时才重新读取，所以一阵事件只会触发一次刷新。刷新按钮随时手动刷新，旁边的标记显示连接是否在线，断了会自己退避重连。

What this is

hookjudge is the judge behind the pipe: one normalized event in, one verdict out (importance, type, a one-sentence summary), returned to the pipe and recorded in the ledger. It renders no cards and knows no channels — that is the pipe's job; it runs no deep investigation — that is the investigator's. The judge answers exactly one question: is this alert worth interrupting a human for.

It also declares which buttons that verdict deserves — silence for a window scaled to how bad it just said the condition is, and the useful/useless pair — because what a button means is judgement. A recovery declares none: there is nothing left to silence. Minting the signed token behind a button and catching the press stay the pipe's, so this service still holds no secret.

Four routes are the cost policy — the order is the policy

RouteWhen it is takenCosts?
recoveryA recovery inherits the verdict its firing was given — the point is agreeing with the alert it belongs to, not merely saving a callfree
reuseThe same condition restated inside the window (1h by default) re-serves the previous AI verdict — an alert storm is one situation repeatedfree
aiA genuinely new condition: ask the modelpaid
ruleKeyword floor when the model is unconfigured, slow or unusable; degraded_reason says why it degraded and never pretends to be analysis. Known levels are carried over conservativelyfree

The saving does not come from a cheaper model. It comes from most events never reaching ai at all.

Reading this board

The sentence at the top of the board says whether anything needs you: verdicts that never reached the pipe first, then disagreements waiting for review. Beside it, the bar is the cost policy: which route every verdict took, and what it cost.

Six numbers under it: verdicts in 24h (the number of times somebody was interrupted), spend and the paid share, failed returns (above 0 means the pipe is not receiving verdicts — with HOOKJUDGE_ALARM_URL set the news travels around the pipe as a direct self-alarm), platform agreement (on shadow deployments the pipe delivers each event with the upstream platform's own verdict as level, so the ledger can say how often the two agree; recoveries are excluded, their importance is inherited by design), repeat interruptions and mattered.

Noisiest conditions is the view to act on — every condition that interrupted more than once, with how many of those interruptions were paid for and how many anyone ruled worth it. A row with several interruptions and nothing ruled useful is where to go turn something off. Nothing here suppresses anything: the judge still returns every verdict.

Verdicts lists every verdict by day — importance on the left, route and time on the right; click a row for the whole of it. Review lists where the platform and the judge disagreed: one click is one eval label, and the labels export.

Attention, which is not the same bill as spend

Every verdict goes back to the pipe and becomes a card, so verdicts has always been the number of times somebody was interrupted — it was only ever read as throughput. A condition judged twelve times inside an hour interrupted a human twelve times, and because eleven of those took the free reuse route the spend figure says nothing about it. Repeat interruptions is that hidden part. Mattered is the other half: when an operator presses a button on a card, useful and useless come back to POST /feedback and are recorded against the judgement that caused the interruption. It is a different question from review: a correctly-rated high can still not be worth waking anyone.

API and addresses

Inside the family's docker network: http://hookjudge:8200. From the host: http://127.0.0.1:8200.

EndpointPurpose
POST /eventsA normalized event from the pipe (timestamped HMAC optional). Answers 202 at once — the verdict travels back through the return door rather than holding the connection
POST /feedbackA human pressed a button on a card, forwarded by the pipe with the channel stripped off it (same signature scheme and secret as /events). useful/useless are recorded against the judgement; silence is a request to the pipe and records no ruling — "make it stop" is not "it did not matter"
GET /status · GET /disagreementsWhat this page reads (read token)
POST /judgements/{id}/label · GET /labels/exportRecord one review label; export them all
GET /liveThis page's wake-up line: one changed per verdict landed, so the page keeps no clock
GET /healthz · GET /metricsLiveness; Prometheus text

The knobs that matter

envMeaning
HOOKJUDGE_AI_BASE_URL / _API_KEY / _MODELAny OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Leave them empty and every event lands on the rule floor — usable but blunt, and reuse can never happen
HOOKJUDGE_REUSE_WINDOW_SECONDSThe restatement window (3600 by default)
HOOKJUDGE_RETURN_URL / _RETURN_SECRETThe one way out — the pipe's judge-notify door. Retried with backoff; out of attempts it dead-letters and self-alarms
HOOKJUDGE_RETENTION_DAYS · HOOKJUDGE_ALARM_URLLedger retention (30 days by default) / where a dead-letter self-alarm goes

This page keeps no clock. It holds one streaming connection to /live and refetches when the service says a verdict landed, so a burst of events is one refetch. The refresh button refetches on demand; the badge beside it says whether the connection is up, and a dropped stream reconnects on its own, backing off.

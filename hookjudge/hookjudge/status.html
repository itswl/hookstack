What this is

hookjudge is the judge behind the pipe: one normalized event in, one verdict out (importance, type, a one-sentence summary), returned to the pipe and recorded in the ledger. It renders no cards and knows no channels — that is the pipe's job; it runs no deep investigation — that is the investigator's. The judge answers exactly one question: is this alert worth interrupting a human for.

It also declares which buttons that verdict deserves — silence for a window scaled to how bad it just said the condition is, and the useful/useless pair — because what a button means is judgement. A recovery declares none: there is nothing left to silence, and whether resolution notices are wanted at all is a channel setting. Minting the signed token behind a button and catching the press stay the pipe's, so this service still holds no secret.

Four routes = the cost policy (the order IS the policy)

RouteWhen it is takenCosts?
recoveryA recovery inherits the verdict its firing was given — the point is agreeing with the alert it belongs to, not merely saving a callfree
reuseThe same condition restated inside the window (1h by default) re-serves the previous AI verdict — an alert storm is one situation repeatedfree
aiA genuinely new condition: ask the modelpaid
ruleKeyword floor when the model is unconfigured, slow or unusable; degraded_reason says why it degraded and never pretends to be analysis. Known levels are carried over conservativelyfree

The saving does not come from a cheaper model. It comes from most events never reaching ai at all.

Reading this board

Seven cards: verdicts in 24h / paid share (the ai fraction) / spend in 24h / failed returns (>0 means the pipe is not receiving verdicts — with HOOKJUDGE_ALARM_URL set, the news travels around the pipe as a direct self-alarm) / platform agreement — on shadow deployments the pipe delivers each event with the upstream platform's own verdict as level, so the ledger can say how often the judge and the platform agree. Recoveries are excluded (their importance is inherited from the firing by design), and the /status JSON carries the full level×importance matrix plus the latest disagreements — the rows worth reading / repeat interruptions and mattered, below. Each verdict row is badged with its route, next to the latency and what that one verdict cost.

Attention, which is not the same bill as spend

Every verdict goes back to the pipe and becomes a card, so verdicts 24h has always been the number of times somebody was interrupted — it was only ever read as throughput. A condition judged twelve times inside an hour interrupted a human twelve times, and because eleven of those took the free reuse route the spend figure says nothing about it. repeat interruptions is that hidden part: cards that restated a condition this window had already reported. mattered is the other half — when an operator presses a button on a card, useful and useless come back to POST /feedback and are recorded against the judgement that caused the interruption. It is a different question from the review queue below, which asks what importance the alert should have had: a correctly-rated high can still not be worth waking anyone, so both answers live on the row.

Noisiest conditions is the view to act on — every condition that interrupted more than once, with how many of those interruptions were paid for and how many anyone ruled worth it. A row with several interruptions and nothing ruled useful is where to go turn something off. Nothing here suppresses anything: the judge still returns every verdict, and who owns noise when a verdict is reused stays an open decision — this only makes the bill legible enough to settle it on evidence.

This page keeps no clock. It holds one streaming connection to /live and refetches what it is showing when the service says a verdict landed, so a burst of events is one refetch rather than a tick each. The header's ↻ refetches on demand, the badge beside it reads live or offline (a dropped stream reconnects on its own, backing off), and the stamp says when the data is from.

API and addresses

Inside the family's docker network: http://hookjudge:8200. From the host: the loopback mapping http://127.0.0.1:8200.

EndpointPurpose
POST /eventsA normalized event from the pipe (timestamped HMAC optional). Answers 202 at once — the verdict travels back through the return door rather than holding the connection
POST /feedbackA human pressed a button on a card, forwarded by the pipe with the channel stripped off it (same signature scheme and secret as /events). useful/useless are recorded against the judgement; silence is a request to the pipe, and this service answers it without recording a ruling — "make it stop" is not "it did not matter"
GET /statusWhat this page reads (X-Read-Token)
GET /healthz · GET /metricsLiveness / Prometheus text

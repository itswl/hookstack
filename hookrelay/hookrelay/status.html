这是什么

hookrelay 是整个 hookstack 唯一的入口，也是唯一的出口。各种监控系统的 webhook 从这里进来，被整理成统一格式、去重、按规则交给判官（hookjudge）和调查员（hookprobe）；它们的结论再从这里做成卡片，发到飞书、钉钉或任何 webhook。它刻意不读告警内容，只对流程负责：每条消息都有账，每次投递都有结果。

这个页面怎么读

告警

顶部一句话告诉你现在有没有事要你处理，旁边是过去 24 小时的活动。下面每一行是一条告警，连同它引出的所有后续：判定、卡片、调查报告、有人按的按钮、恢复和修复结论。左边的标签说它现在的状态：

标签意思要你做什么
待处理卡片提了问，还没人按去飞书里按，或者点开看看
处理中已经交给判官或调查员，还没回音；或者修复已批准，在等条件回应不用管，有结果会变色
已恢复 · 修复已生效告警条件结束了，或者批准的修复起作用了不用管
投递失败 · 修复未生效有渠道没收到，或者修复后条件又出现了需要看一下
已通知该发的都发了，没有需要你回应的不用管
已标记有用有人按过卡片上的按钮，标签就是按的那一个，比如已批准、已追问不用管

每行右下角的小圆点是七个阶段：收到、判定、通知、调查、人工、恢复、修复。实心的是发生过的，黄色是在等人，绿色是好结果，红色是坏结果，空心的是没发生。没有部署判官的环境不显示"判定"。心跳、重复、静默期内这类安静事件默认不显示，勾选"显示安静事件"就能看到。

点任意一行，右侧会滑出这条告警的完整经过：每个阶段发生了什么、调查报告全文、改动 diff、受阻项和建议步骤，还有每一次投递、原始内容和审计记录。按 Esc 或点空白处关闭，浏览器的后退键也能关。

流水

按事件原样记账的查账视图：每条事件逐道闸门的决策过程、投到了哪里、回来了什么。排查"为什么这条没发出去"时用它。

投递

各渠道的送达情况、放弃重试的投递（可以一键重试）、熔断状态，以及"没人打开的卡片"：页面会去问飞书桥哪些卡片还没人看，只在你点按钮时才问。

静默

正在生效的静默和新建静默。被静默的事件仍然会记账，只是不投递。在告警详情里也可以直接静默它的来源。

路由

入口、闸门、出口组成的流转图，结构上的隐患会在最上面标出来；下面是配置文件编辑器和试运行。这一页需要管理口令。

搜索

在顶部搜索框里输入文字，会按标题筛选告警。粘贴一个事件号（如 742）、关联号（hr-742）、会话（probe:watch:742）、工作号或飞书卡片号（om_ 开头）再按回车，会直接打开那条告警。

口令

看数据需要 读取口令，也就是 hookrelay 的 HOOKRELAY_READ_TOKEN；改东西（静默、重试、改路由、试运行）需要 管理口令，也就是 HOOKRELAY_ADMIN_TOKEN。服务端没设读取口令时，页面不用口令也能看；没设管理口令时，页面上的写操作是关闭的。口令只保存在你这个浏览器里，可以在右上角的设置里修改或清除。work 部署里，这两个值分别来自 .env 的 WORK_READ_TOKEN 和 WORK_ADMIN_TOKEN。

外观

页面默认跟随系统的浅色或深色设置。点刷新按钮旁边的太阳或月亮可以切换；切回和系统一样的那一种，就恢复跟随系统。右上角的设置里也可以直接选。这个选择只保存在当前浏览器里，三个看板各记各的。

接口速查

在 docker 网络里用 http://hookrelay:8100，在本机用 http://127.0.0.1:8100。读接口带 X-Read-Token，管理接口带 X-Admin-Token。

接口用途
POST /hook/{source}入口。每个来源在 config.yaml 里声明签名、提取模板和级别映射
GET /status · GET /timeline这个页面读的数据：最近的事件及其决策和投递；同样的事件按告警归成链
GET /trace/{id} · GET /audit/{id}一条告警的完整经过，包括双向字节；{id} 可以是事件号、hr-…、会话、工作号或卡片号。以及同一操作的审计记录，只含摘要
GET /unseen没人打开的卡片，现问飞书桥（读取口令）
GET /live页面的更新通知：账本每次写入推一条 changed，所以页面不用定时刷新
POST /explain/{source} · GET /topology试运行：这条内容会被送到哪里；以及整张路由图（管理口令）
GET/PUT /config · POST /config/reload读取、替换、从文件重载配置（管理口令）
POST /silences · DELETE /silences/{id}新建和解除静默（管理口令）
POST /deliveries/{id}/retry让一条放弃了的投递再试一次（管理口令）
POST /card-action卡片按钮的回调，令牌签名、只能用一次
GET /metrics · GET /healthzPrometheus 指标（读取口令）；存活检查

配置要点（config.yaml）

概念一句话
sources每个入口的签名密钥、提取模板和 level_map。配置里的 ${NAME} 从 .env 展开
routes顺序就是含义：按优先级从高到低累积匹配，stop: true 截断。回流路由优先级最高并且截断，结果不会被处理第二次
channelspayload: normalized 发标准事件给节点；processed 把节点的结论做成卡片；raw 原样转发
降噪指纹去重、静默、风暴熔断（只看量，不看内容）
保留期HOOKRELAY_RETENTION_DAYS，默认 14 天

管道自己出问题时，死信自报警会绕开整个栈，直接发到 HOOKRELAY_ALARM_URL。

What this is

hookrelay is the family's only front door for alerts, and its only way out. Webhooks from every monitoring system arrive here, are normalized, de-duplicated and routed to the judge (hookjudge) and the investigator (hookprobe); what they conclude comes back through here and is dressed as a card for Feishu, DingTalk or any webhook. It is deliberately content-blind and answers for the mechanics: every message accounted for, every delivery with an outcome.

Reading this page

Alerts

The sentence at the top says whether anything needs you, beside a day of activity. Each row below is one alert and everything that followed it: the verdict, the cards, the investigation, the buttons a person pressed, the recovery and the verdict on a fix. The label on the left is where it stands now:

LabelMeansWhat to do
Needs youa card asked something and nobody pressedpress it in the chat, or open the alert
In flighthanded to the judge or the investigator, nothing back yet; or a fix was approved and the condition has not answerednothing yet — it changes colour when it lands
Resolved · Fix heldthe condition ended, or the approved fix workednothing
Delivery failed · Fix did not holda channel did not receive it, or the condition came back after the fixtake a look
Notifiedeverything that should go out went out, nothing asks for younothing
Ruled usefulsomebody pressed a button on the card; the label names which one, such as Approved or Asked whynothing

The small dots are seven stages: received, judged, notified, investigated, person, condition, fix. Filled means it happened, amber waits on a person, green is a good outcome, red a bad one, hollow did not happen. A deployment without a judge does not show the judged stage. Quiet events — ticks, repeats, anything inside a silence — are hidden unless you tick “Show quiet events”.

Click any row and its whole story slides in from the right: what each stage did, the full report, the change as a diff, what blocked a step and what was proposed, every delivery, the raw bytes and the audit record. Esc, a click outside or the browser's back button closes it.

Ledger

The forensic view: every event as recorded, its decision gate by gate, where it went and what came back. The place to answer “why did this one not go out”.

Deliveries

How each channel is doing, the deliveries that gave up (retry them in one click), the circuit breakers, and the cards nobody opened — asked of the chat bridge only when you press the button.

Silences

The silences in force, and a form for a new one. Silenced events still land in the ledger; only their delivery stops. An alert's story can silence its source directly.

Routing

Doors, gates and exits, with the structural hazards named at the top; then the config editor and a dry run. Needs the admin token.

Search

Type in the search box to filter alerts by title. Paste an event id (742), a correlation (hr-742), a session (probe:watch:742), a work id or a card's platform id (om_…) and press Enter to open that alert directly.

Tokens

Reading needs the read token, the pipe's HOOKRELAY_READ_TOKEN; changing anything — silences, retries, routing, dry runs — needs the admin token, HOOKRELAY_ADMIN_TOKEN. With no read token set on the pipe the page reads without one; with no admin token set, changes from this page are switched off. Tokens stay in this browser only; change or forget them under Settings, top right. On the work deployment they come from WORK_READ_TOKEN and WORK_ADMIN_TOKEN in .env.

Keyboard

/ search · j next · k previous · Enter open · Esc close · ? shortcuts

Appearance

The page follows the system's light or dark setting. The sun or moon beside refresh switches it, and switching back to what the system uses returns to following the system. Settings, top right, has the same choice. The pick is kept in this browser only, and each of the three boards keeps its own.

API quick reference

Inside the family's docker network use http://hookrelay:8100; from the host, http://127.0.0.1:8100. Read endpoints take X-Read-Token, admin endpoints X-Admin-Token.

EndpointPurpose
POST /hook/{source}The front door. Each source is declared in config.yaml with its signature, templates and level map
GET /status · GET /timelineWhat this page reads: recent events with their decisions and deliveries; the same events gathered into chains
GET /trace/{id} · GET /audit/{id}One alert's whole journey, bytes of both directions included — {id} is an event id, hr-…, a session key, a work id or a card's platform id; and the same operation as an accountability record with digests instead of bytes
GET /unseenCards nobody opened, asked of the chat bridge on demand (read token)
GET /liveThis page's wake-up line: one changed per ledger write, so the page keeps no clock
POST /explain/{source} · GET /topologyDry run: where this payload would go; and the whole routing graph (admin)
GET/PUT /config · POST /config/reloadRead, replace and hot-reload the config (admin)
POST /silences · DELETE /silences/{id}The silence valve (admin)
POST /deliveries/{id}/retryA dead letter's second chance (admin)
POST /card-actionA card button's callback, signed and single-use
GET /metrics · GET /healthzPrometheus text (read token); liveness

The config that matters (config.yaml)

ConceptIn one line
sourcesPer door: the signing secret, extraction templates and level_map. A ${NAME} in the config expands from .env
routesOrder is meaning: matches accumulate from the highest priority down and stop: true truncates. Return routes sit highest and stop, so a result is never processed twice
channelspayload: normalized sends the standard event to a node; processed dresses a node's result as a card; raw passes the original through
Noise controlFingerprint dedup, silences, and the storm fuse (volume, never content)
RetentionHOOKRELAY_RETENTION_DAYS, 14 by default

When the pipe itself is what broke, the dead-letter self-alarm posts straight to HOOKRELAY_ALARM_URL, around the stack that just failed.
